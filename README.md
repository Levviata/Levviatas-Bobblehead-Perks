# Levviata's SPECIAL (a 1.12.2 forge mod)
_Nuclear Tech addon_

Consume Nuclear Tech's bobbleheads with crouch right-click to get SPECIAL and perks.

## Features
- Consume any bobblehead to get stats (fallout bobbles give SPECIAL while other augment the current stat limit), like:
  - Strength, on-hit attack damage
  - Perception, increases gun damage
  - Endurance, increases max health, has a bonus at level 5 which gives amplified regeneration
  - Charisma, increases armor and armor toughness. At level 5 it gives the resistance effect
  - Intelligence, increases fire rate, reduces gun wear, and gives infinite item durability. At level 5 makes the Pepperbox shot 6 rounds at once, at level 10 makes revolvers penetrate and deal x3 headshot damage
  - Agility, gives speed which can be configured with /setspeed
  - Luck, chance to refund a magazine on reload
- Bobbleheads renamed, now have their stats on their display name
- Extensive configuration, modify literally anything. Includes advanced modification of any stat's logic
- Bobblehead recipes to reduce grinding, with any non-fallout bobblehead and ingredients
- Stat GUI on press key P
